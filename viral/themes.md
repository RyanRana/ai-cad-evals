# 5 angles, 5 audiences

Each variant is the same product positioned for a different feed. Pick
the one that matches where you're posting and you'll dominate that
audience; mix and match to A/B test on alts.

| # | Theme | Hook | Best on | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Contrarian finding** | "everyone ships, nobody evaluates — most can't make a flange" | X main, LinkedIn | none — the flange line is universal |
| 02 | **Numbers-led** | "10 agents · 308 prompts · 20 categories · 0 ship-ready" | HN, X power users | reads dry to non-engineers |
| 03 | **Tech flex** | "IRT 2PL ability + p5 floor + Pareto frontier" | ML/research X, arxiv-sanity crowd | jargon turns away mainstream |
| 04 | **Builder personal** | "I got annoyed at AI CAD demos so I spent two weekends evaluating them" | X founder loop, IndieHackers | only works if your handle has any reach |
| 05 | **Thread starter** | "the current state of AI CAD: a thread" | X (only) | requires you to actually thread it out |

## Where each theme wins

- **HN** rewards numerical specificity + a contrarian finding. Use 02 for
  the title, 01's flange line for the lead comment.
- **X** rewards a tight 1-line hook with a screenshot. Use 01 for the
  post and 03 in a quote-tweet/reply if it gets traction.
- **LinkedIn** rewards the founder narrative. Use 04 verbatim.
- **arXiv-sanity / r/MachineLearning** like 02 + 03 combined into a
  one-paragraph abstract. Don't oversell.

## What never works

- Saying "the future of CAD" or "revolutionizing X" — instant downvote.
- Hashtag spam. One niche hashtag is fine; three+ kills reach.
- Posting at 2am PT. HN front page is decided by 8–10am PT Tues / Wed.
- Replying defensively to negative HN comments. State your reasoning,
  link to /methodology, move on.

## The cardinal rule

The flange line is the asset. Whatever theme you pick, "most can't make
a flange" is the line that travels. Don't bury it.
