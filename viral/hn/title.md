# Hacker News title

HN allows up to **80 chars**. Each option below is verified within
limit. The first one is the recommended pick.

## 🥇 Top pick (75 chars)

```
Show HN: I tested 10 AI CAD tools on 308 prompts. Most can't make a flange
```

Why it wins:
- "Show HN:" is mandatory.
- First-person + concrete numbers = real personal project, not marketing.
- The contrarian finding is the hook. Both engineers ("a flange?
  really?") and AI people ("are CAD agents that bad?") will click.
- No buzzwords. HN's title-cleansing instinct rewards plain.

## Alternates

```
Show HN: CADBench – 308 prompts, 10 agents. Most AI CAD tools fail at flanges    (78)
Show HN: I scored Zoo, Claude, GPT-5, Gemini, Trellis on 308 CAD tasks            (74)
Show HN: A research-grade benchmark for AI CAD agents (most fail at flanges)      (78)

```

## Don't

- **Don't** name a product like a startup ("CADBench: the Future of …"). HN
  hates startup-speak in titles.
- **Don't** include emojis or all-caps. Insta-down-rank.
- **Don't** post anonymously / via a fresh account. Use your real handle
  with karma — it matters.
